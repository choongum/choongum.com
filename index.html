    Bio

    Choongum makes electronic music for both ends of the night. Some records are ambient and downtempo, others are trancey and built for dancing, and trip hop, hyphy and hip hop keep turning up underneath. Whatever the tempo, it tends to come out melodic, a little loose and bright.

    His DJ sets go the same way, from the chill-out room to the club floor and back again.

    When he's not making music, he paints and takes photos of the places he travels through.
